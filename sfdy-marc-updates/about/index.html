About

What Europe fields in space has to count.

Europe depends on space for communication, navigation and observation. It will not field the largest fleets in orbit, so every system it does field must be dependable.

That shapes how we build space systems for Europe's defence. We use what the market offers and develop what it does not.

How we work

01

We are user-driven

We work with end users from the start, so that requirements come directly from their operational needs, not from us.

02

We test early

We build and test early in development. Practice emerges through testing, and faults show while they are cheap to fix.

03

We partner up

Each development runs with partners drawn from across the institutional and industrial ecosystem, working together throughout.
