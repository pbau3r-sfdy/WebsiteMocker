Hypersonics: a distinct domain

At speeds beyond Mach 5, extreme aerodynamic heating renders conventional aerospace technologies obsolete. These regimes require state-of-the-art materials, control architectures and avionic components to ensure stable and manoeuvrable hypersonic flight.

Hypersonica was founded to address these challenges and establish the capability to rapidly design, test and field systems that perform reliably under such extreme conditions.
