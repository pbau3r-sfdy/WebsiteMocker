We aim to equip European allies with a first-of-kind, sovereign, cost effective hypersonic strike capability

Who we are

We believe strongly in European values and our way of life. We also believe that the only response to threats to our democracies is strength. Hypersonica is committed to strengthening Europe's independent defence capabilities while advancing technologies that drive prosperity and innovation. As Europeans, we pursue this work with a clear sense of responsibility for the safe and principled development of this cutting-edge technology.
