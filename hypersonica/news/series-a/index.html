10th February 2026 · Munich – London

Hypersonica raises €23.3 million Series A to deliver Europe's first sovereign hypersonic strike capability

Hypersonica has closed a €23.3 million Series A funding round, led by leading European defence and deep tech investors. The round will fund accelerated development and testing of Hypersonica's sovereign hypersonic strike capability.

The raise brings Hypersonica's total funding to date to over €28 million, with backing from Plural, SPRIND and General Catalyst, among others.

Europe's sovereign strike capability

"This funding will allow us to accelerate our flight test programme and scale our engineering team," said Dr. Marc Ewenz, Co-founder and CTO. "We are on track to deliver Europe's first sovereign hypersonic strike capability."

The company currently employs a team of engineers in its Munich headquarters and London subsidiary, with plans to double headcount within the next 12 months.

Investor perspectives

"Hypersonica is building exactly the kind of sovereign capability Europe needs right now," said Ian Hogarth, Partner at Plural. "We are proud to lead this round."
