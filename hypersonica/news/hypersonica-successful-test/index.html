Hypersonica successfully tests Europe's sovereign hypersonic missile prototype

Hypersonica has successfully conducted the first flight test of its sovereign European hypersonic missile prototype at the Andøya Space launch facility in Norway.

The test marks a critical milestone for European defence autonomy, demonstrating that a European company can develop, build and fly a hypersonic vehicle entirely within European borders using European talent and technology.

A sovereign European capability

The vehicle achieved hypersonic speeds during a controlled flight test, validating key aerodynamic, propulsion and guidance systems. The test was conducted within Norwegian airspace in close coordination with national authorities.

"This is not just a technical achievement — it is proof that Europe can develop the sovereign strike capability it needs to deter aggression," said Dr. Philipp Kerth, Co-founder and CEO of Hypersonica.

What comes next

Hypersonica will now proceed to an accelerated programme of follow-on tests, refining vehicle performance across a wider envelope of conditions. The company continues to expand its engineering team and manufacturing capabilities in Munich and London.
