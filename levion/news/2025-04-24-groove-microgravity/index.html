GROOVE: From Microgravity Research to Commercial Application

We are pleased to introduce 'GROOVE' — our current project within the ESA BSGN Business in Space Growth Network Advanced Materials and In-orbit Manufacturing Industry Accelerator.

GROOVE (Growth Parameter Observation & Validation Experiment) is our first mission in space, conducted as part of the ESA Business in Space Growth Network accelerator program.

The goal is to validate the core parameters of our crystal growth process under real orbital microgravity conditions. This data forms the scientific foundation for our commercial crystal growth service.

Why GROOVE Matters

Crystal growth in orbital microgravity eliminates the convective forces that cause defects in Earth-grown crystals. The GROOVE mission allows us to observe and characterize this process in the actual environment where our commercial service will operate.

What We Are Measuring

During the GROOVE mission, we monitor key growth parameters including:

  • Temperature gradients across the crystal growth interface
  • Growth rates under varying thermal conditions
  • Crystal homogeneity and defect distribution
  • Process stability over extended durations

The data gathered through GROOVE will directly inform the design of our commercial crystal growth platform, ensuring that our service delivers the material quality our customers require.

Part of the ESA BSGN Accelerator

The ESA Business in Space Growth Network (BSGN) Advanced Materials and In-orbit Manufacturing Industry Accelerator supports companies developing commercial applications for space-based manufacturing. Being part of this program gives Levion Materials access to ESA’s network of space infrastructure providers, potential customers, and technical expertise.

We are grateful for the support of ESA and the BSGN community in making GROOVE possible.
