The Future of Orbital Manufacturing: Launching Levion Materials

Demand has accelerated across both core business areas, with strong pull from their respective markets, with distinct customer needs, partner ecosystems, and success factors.

Following the growth in demand for both high-quality crystal materials and orbital manufacturing capabilities, we are formally establishing Levion Materials as a dedicated entity focused on crystal growth in space.

Our two core product lines serve distinct markets with complementary technology requirements, allowing us to address a wide range of customer needs efficiently.

Why Now

The convergence of accessible orbital infrastructure, advances in crystal growth technology, and increasing demand for ultra-pure materials has created a unique window of opportunity. Customers across the semiconductor, photonics, and quantum technology sectors are actively seeking materials with properties that Earth-based manufacturing cannot reliably deliver.

Crystal Growth as a Service

Levion Materials offers crystal growth in orbital microgravity as a service. Customers specify their material requirements — composition, dimensions, purity levels — and we handle the end-to-end process: mission planning, growth execution, and material return.

Our target materials include:

  • Semiconductor substrates for power electronics
  • Optical crystals for laser systems and photonic applications
  • Scintillator crystals for radiation detection
  • Quantum materials for next-generation computing

The Team Behind Levion

Levion Materials brings together expertise in materials science, space systems engineering, and commercial space operations. Our founding team has direct experience in crystal growth research, orbital payload integration, and space-derived material characterization.

We are building the infrastructure and processes that will make orbital crystal growth a reliable, scalable commercial service.

What Comes Next

Our immediate focus is on the GROOVE mission — our first orbital validation experiment — followed by the development of our commercial growth platform. We welcome conversations with potential customers, partners, and investors who share our conviction that the future of advanced materials manufacturing extends beyond Earth.

Reach us at lab@levion-materials.com.
