THE PROBLEM

The Problem with Gravity

On Earth, gravity constantly acts on molten materials during crystal growth.

  • Uneven composition during solidification
  • Internal mechanical stress
  • Crystallographic defects from convective forces
  • Inability to reach macroscopically level atomic alignment

Even in the best laboratories, gravity cannot be switched off.

THE SOLUTION

The Change with Microgravity

In microgravity, materials behave differently — no sedimentation, no buoyancy-driven convection, more uniform growth. Crystals can form in a way that is impossible on Earth.

  • Stable composition
  • Fewer structural defects
  • More uniform growth
