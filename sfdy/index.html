Technologies for the High Frontier

We build forEurope's defence in space.

Munich · Vilnius

We enable European in-space power projection.

Starflight Dynamics is a European space company. We design systems that operate close to other objects in orbit, to see, inspect and protect what matters in space. Persistent and dependable, our systems are built with their users for real operational use. From our German anchor, working with German institutions, we grow towards our European partners and beyond.

What we build, and where

Munich Propulsion Dual-mode, water-based propulsion that combines fast manoeuvres and long endurance in one technology. Munich Payloads The equipment that enables our missions, built for our own platforms and ready to integrate on others. Munich Platforms High-endurance platforms, producible at scale, built around our own propulsion and payloads. Vilnius Mission preparation Simulation to plan and rehearse missions, from first concept to the final run before flight.
