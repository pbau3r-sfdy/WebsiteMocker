Mission & responsibilities

You will shape the next generations of SFDY space platforms, exploring the concepts, orbits and architectures that come after OVERWATCH.

Concept studies & sizing

  1. Lead concept and feasibility studies for future platforms and missions, from first idea to a sized design
  2. Own preliminary sizing and system budgets for new concepts, and the models and tools behind them
  3. Explore new operating regimes, including very low Earth orbit (VLEO), and the technologies they demand

Architecture & technology

  1. Run architecture trade-offs across platform, propulsion, payload and mission, including alternative technologies and approaches
  2. Assess emerging technologies and turn them into a roadmap for future SFDY systems
  3. Feed lessons from OVERWATCH and current programmes into the next designs

Proposals & collaboration

  1. Turn studies into proposals and technical content for ESA, BAAINBw, DLR and other customers
  2. Work with concept development in Vilnius to tie future systems to operational needs

What we are looking for

Must have

  • 5-10 years in spacecraft systems engineering, mission analysis or concept design
  • Strong in sizing, budgets and trade-offs, with your own modelling tools (e.g. Python, MATLAB)
  • Curiosity for unconventional solutions, matched by engineering rigour
  • Fluent English

Nice to have

  • German
  • Experience with VLEO, drag compensation or air-breathing propulsion
  • Experience with concurrent-engineering studies (e.g. ESA CDF style)
  • Experience writing technical proposals
Eligibility

Willingness and eligibility to undergo a security clearance check (Sicherheitsüberprüfung, SÜG) may be required for this role, depending on project assignment.

About Starflight Dynamics

Starflight Dynamics (SFDY) is a Munich-based SpaceTech company building the foundational technologies for Europe's next generation of orbital infrastructure. Founded in 2023, we develop dual-use systems for space and defence: a green, water-based orbital propulsion stack and modular platforms for in-orbit operations.

Working with partners such as the University of the German Armed Forces in Munich and the BAAINBw, we're a small, ambitious team moving fast from prototype to orbit. Join us and help shape how Europe operates, and defends its interests, in space.
