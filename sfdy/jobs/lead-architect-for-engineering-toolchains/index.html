Mission & responsibilities

You will turn the way SFDY builds engineering tools into a company capability: standing on the shoulders of validated tools, and putting what we build into everyone’s hands.

Architecture & platform

  1. Own the architecture of SFDY’s engineering-toolchain platform: shared infrastructure, data contracts, interfaces and access for every team
  2. Re-architect our first prototype tools into maintained company services
  3. Integrate validated toolboxes from trusted sources rather than reinventing them

Standards & verification

  1. Set the standards that make our tools trustworthy: verification against reference cases, traceable sources, code review and documentation
  2. Establish automated testing, releases and reproducibility for every tool on the platform
  3. Make sure our tools respect licensing, export-control and information-security rules, together with our IT & Information Security Lead

Practice & enablement

  1. Build the practice that lets engineers turn their own problems into tools, using AI-assisted development responsibly
  2. Coach engineers in Munich and Vilnius, and grow a small toolchain team
  3. Decide which engineering problems to codify next, based on where the company gains most

What we are looking for

Must have

  • 8+ years in scientific or engineering software, including architecting shared platforms
  • A background in engineering or physics, ideally space or aerospace: you can judge whether a result is right, not just whether the code runs
  • Experience with verification and validation of engineering or simulation software
  • Hands-on experience with AI-assisted software development, and a clear view of where it needs guardrails
  • Fluent English

Nice to have

  • German or Lithuanian
  • Experience with established space-engineering and mission-analysis tools
  • Experience with concurrent-design or model-based systems engineering (MBSE) environments
  • Experience running software in security-sensitive or defence environments

About Starflight Dynamics

Starflight Dynamics (SFDY) is a Munich-based SpaceTech company building the foundational technologies for Europe's next generation of orbital infrastructure. Founded in 2023, we develop dual-use systems for space and defence: a green, water-based orbital propulsion stack and modular platforms for in-orbit operations.

Working with partners such as the University of the German Armed Forces in Munich and the BAAINBw, we're a small, ambitious team moving fast from prototype to orbit. Join us and help shape how Europe operates, and defends its interests, in space.

Employer for this position: Starflight Dynamics UAB.
