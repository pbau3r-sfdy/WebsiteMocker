Privacy Policy & Data Protection

At Starflight Dynamics (SFDY), we are committed to protecting your privacy and personal information. The protection of your privacy when processing personal data is an important concern for us. Therefore, we collect, store, and use your personal data strictly in accordance with applicable data protection regulations, including, without limitation, the European General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

We do not publish or share your information with third parties without your permission.

Personal data

Personal data are any information which are related to an identified or identifiable natural person, such as name, e-mail address, postal address (Art. 4 (1) GDPR). Any information that cannot be directly linked to your identity (e.g. statistical details) is not considered to be personal data.

You can use our website without disclosing your identity and without providing any personal data.

Whenever personal data is collected on our website, you will only be asked to provide the data that is absolutely necessary and on a voluntary basis. Insofar as we temporarily store or process your personal data, this shall be done on the basis of Art. 6 (1) GDPR and national data protection provisions (BDSG).

If you have any questions concerning our privacy policy, or the processing and collecting data through our website, please contact our data protection officer: Dr. Philipp Bauer, CEO, mission-control@starflight-dynamics.com

Server log file

When you visit this website, the browser used on your terminal device sends information to our website server automatically. This information is temporarily stored in a so-called "log file". The following information is collected without any action by you and is stored until its automatic deletion:

  • The IP address of the inquiring PC
  • The access date and time
  • The name and the URL of the recalled file
  • The address of the loading page (referrer URL)
  • The browser in use and (if necessary) your PC operating system, as well as the name of your access provider
  • Access status (e.g., file transmitted, file not found, etc.)
  • Transferred amount of data

Legal basis for data processing is Art. 6 Par. 1-1-f) of the GDPR.

Cookies policy

Our website uses cookies. These are small text files that are stored on your device and are used to make websites work more efficiently by saving your preferences. Cookies cannot harm your terminal device and contain no virus, Trojan horse or other malware.

By changing the settings in your Internet browser, you can disable or restrict the transfer of cookies. Previously stored cookies can be deleted at any time. If cookies are disabled for our website, you might not be able to fully use all the website's functions.

The legal basis for collecting or processing personal data by means of cookies is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR in conjunction with Section 3 of the BDSG and/or Article 6 (1) point (a) of the GDPR for cookies which require consent.

Data transmission

We do not transmit your personal data to any third party for purposes different from the ones listed below. We transmit your personal data to third parties only if:

  • You have granted your specific consent according to Art. 6 Par. 1-1-a) of the GDPR
  • The transmission is necessary for asserting, exercising or safeguarding rights according to Art. 6 Par. 1-1-f) of the GDPR
  • The transmission is compulsory by law according to Art. 6 Par. 1-1-c) of the GDPR
  • This is permitted by law and necessary to contractual relationships with you according to Art. 6 Par. 1-1-b) of the GDPR

Rights of the person concerned

Please contact us if you wish to know which kind of data of yours we store and/or if you want to have it amended or deleted. Moreover, you have the right of processing restriction (Art. 18 of the GDPR), opposition to processing (Art. 21 of the GDPR) and data portability (Art. 20 of the GDPR).

Right of opposition

If your personal data is processed for a legitimate interest according to Art. 6 Par. 1-1-f) of the GDPR, you have the right to oppose the processing of your personal data according to Art. 21 of the GDPR. Please send us an e-mail (see Imprint) in order to exercise your right of withdrawal or opposition.

Data safety

We have implemented all the appropriate technical and administrative safety measures to protect your personal data against loss, destruction, manipulation, and unauthorized access. All of our employees and suppliers are bound by data protection laws. Whenever we collect and process any pieces of personal data, we encrypt them before transmitting them.

Changes to this privacy declaration

We reserve the right to modify our privacy policy declarations as required by new technologies. Please ensure that you always have access to the latest version. In the event of substantial changes to fundamental sections of this Privacy Policy Declaration, we will inform you via our website.
