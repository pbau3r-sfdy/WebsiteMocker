GROOVE — Growth Parameter Observation & Validation Experiment — is Levion's first
in-space materials mission. It runs inside the CGF-XS, the smallest member of the
Crystal Growth Facility family, and exists to replace our growth model with flight data.

## What the mission does

The CGF-XS carries semiconductor feedstock to orbit, melts it at 1.300°C and grows a
1" Ø ingot in true microgravity. Growth parameters are observed from the ground
throughout the run, with „human-in-the-loop" decision points at the critical phases.

## Why it matters

On Earth, buoyancy-driven convection puts defects into compound semiconductors: up to
40% of grown CZT is scrapped. In orbit that convection disappears. GROOVE is the
experiment that turns that expectation into measured evidence.
