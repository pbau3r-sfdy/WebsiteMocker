## The role

Travelling through hyperspace ain't like dusting crops. As Deep Space Navigator you plan trajectories, manoeuvres and launch windows for our missions. This is a remote role with regular trips to Munich.

## Responsibilities

### Trajectory design

1. Design transfer orbits and manoeuvre plans.
2. Optimise delta-v budgets with Propulsion.

### Operations support

1. Support mission rehearsals and live manoeuvre planning.
2. Maintain our flight dynamics tooling.

## Requirements

- MSc in astrodynamics or aerospace engineering.
- Experience with mission analysis tools.
- Self-driven in a remote setting.
- Fluent English.
