## The role

You have built your own lightsaber, passed the Trials and survived at least one catastrophic test-stand failure with your eyebrows intact. Now it is time to pass on what you have learned. As a Jedi Master in Munich you lead small mission teams, mentor Padawans and act as the calm voice in the control room when the telemetry starts doing interesting things.

## Responsibilities

### Mentorship

1. Take on one or two Padawans and own their training plan, progress reviews and occasional existential crises.
2. Teach the difference between "the Force is strong with this one" and "this one needs a code review".
3. Prevent Padawans from becoming too attached to their first prototype.

### Missions

1. Lead cross-functional missions from kickoff to closeout, including the parts with spreadsheets.
2. Represent the Order in technical reviews with partners, customers and the occasional skeptical Senator.
3. Sense disturbances in the schedule before they become disturbances in the budget.

### Order and process

1. Uphold the Jedi Code and our engineering standards, which are surprisingly compatible.
2. Contribute to the Archives: if it is not documented, it does not exist.

## Requirements

- 8+ years of mission experience in aerospace, defence or an adjacent galaxy.
- Proven track record of mentoring juniors without losing a hand in the process.
- Calm under pressure, including blaster fire, design reviews and Monday morning stand-ups.
- Fluent English; German is a strong plus, Huttese is tolerated.
- A healthy suspicion of anyone who says "I have a bad feeling about this" and then does it anyway.
