## The role

Somebody wired R2-D2, and he still works after decades of abuse. You design, build and test flight electronics: power distribution, onboard computers and the harnesses that connect it all.

## Responsibilities

### Design

1. Design PCBs and electronic units for flight hardware.
2. Select radiation-tolerant components and run derating analyses.

### Integration & test

1. Build and test engineering and qualification models.
2. Support EMC and environmental test campaigns.

## Requirements

- Degree in electrical engineering.
- 3+ years designing electronics, ideally for space or aviation.
- Experience with Altium or KiCad.
- Fluent English; German is a plus.
