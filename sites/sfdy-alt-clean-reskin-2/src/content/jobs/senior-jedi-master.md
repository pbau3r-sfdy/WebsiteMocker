## The role

The Order is growing and so are its responsibilities: two sites, a long list of missions and more Padawans than ever. The Senior Jedi Master sits on the Council and owns how the Order works across Munich and Vilnius. You set direction, balance the Force between both sites and make the calls that are too big for a single mission team. Wisdom is the main requirement. Ancient age is optional.

## Responsibilities

### Council leadership

1. Set the Order's strategy and translate prophecies into roadmaps with milestones, owners and budgets.
2. Chair the Council and ensure decisions are made by consensus, or at least by someone.
3. Bring balance to the Force between Munich and Vilnius, one cross-site roadmap meeting at a time.

### People

1. Grow the next generation of Masters and decide who is ready for the Trials.
2. Own hiring for the Order, with a firm rule against recruiting from the Sith Academy.
3. Recognise early signs of the Dark Side, such as unchecked scope creep or hoarding test data.

### External relations

1. Represent the Order to the Senate, customers, funding bodies and the press.
2. Negotiate treaties with partners, written down and countersigned this time.

## Requirements

- 15+ years in leadership roles across engineering and operations, ideally spanning several star systems or at least two countries.
- Experience running a distributed organisation without a holonet outage ending in chaos.
- Proven ability to deliver hard truths gently, including "the prophecy was misread".
- Fluent English; German and Lithuanian are each a strong plus. Speaking backwards in riddles is not required.
- Owns at least one green lightsaber, matching our brand accent.
