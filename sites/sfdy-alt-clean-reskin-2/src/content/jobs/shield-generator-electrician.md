## The role

The shield generator on Endor was guarded by a single squad. Ours has you. You maintain electrical installations for our lab, workshop and test infrastructure, part-time.

## Responsibilities

### Installations

1. Install and maintain electrical systems for labs and test equipment.
2. Perform DGUV V3 inspections and keep records.

### Support

1. Support test campaigns with power and grounding setups.
2. Advise engineers on safe electrical design.

## Requirements

- Qualified electrician (Elektroniker or equivalent).
- Experience in industrial or lab environments.
- Safety-first mindset.
- German required; English is a plus.
