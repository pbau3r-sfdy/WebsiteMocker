## The role

Brings dedicated, accountable leadership to the Propulsion department for the first time. This is a new role, distinct from the propulsion team's current hands-on technical work — the Chief Propulsion Engineer reports to this role but continues to own technical authority on system design and testing, while this role owns the department's strategic direction, people leadership, budget, day-to-day operational and project management (including change and risk management), hiring plan, and delivery accountability to the CEO.

## Responsibilities

### Department leadership

1. Own the overall management of the department, covering day-to-day operational management as well as project, change and risk management.
2. Own the department's strategic direction, set out in its technology and product-line roadmap, and translate it, jointly with the Chief Propulsion Engineer, into concrete mid-term plans.
3. Establish and steer the department's cooperation with other departments as cross-department work grows.
4. Own delivery accountability for the Propulsion department against all external and internal commitments.
5. Build out the department's hiring plan and set department-level priorities, budget and resource allocation.

### Technical and scientific oversight

1. Partner with the Chief Propulsion Engineer on system-level technical decisions, providing an independent, critical second opinion grounded in the current state of the art.
2. Represent Propulsion in company-level technical reviews and roadmap planning.
3. Review and contribute to research publications and technical reports, and track the scientific and technical state of the art.
4. Lead the department's proposal and funding work (e.g. ESA, BAAINBw).

### People & process

1. Establish the department's engineering processes, documentation standards, and onboarding for new hires.
2. Own performance management, personnel education and training, and career development for the department.
3. Be accountable for personnel safety and security in scope of the propulsion department.

## Requirements

- 10-15 years in propulsion or a closely adjacent hardware discipline, including people-leadership experience.
- Track record building or scaling a technical team from a small base.
- Comfortable operating alongside an existing strong technical authority (the Chief Propulsion Engineer) without needing to own every technical call personally.
- Scientific depth to critically assess research and engineering work in the field, typically an advanced degree (MSc or PhD) in aerospace engineering, physics or a related discipline, ideally with a publication record.
- Demonstrated experience in operational and project management, including change and risk management and supervising delegated management functions.
- Fluent English; German is a strong plus for BAAINBw-facing work.
