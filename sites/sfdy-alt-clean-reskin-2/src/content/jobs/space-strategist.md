## The role

It is a trap — unless you saw it coming. As Space Strategist you analyse the European and global space defence landscape, shape our product and market strategy and brief leadership on where the galaxy is heading. You turn geopolitics, budgets and technology trends into decisions.

## Responsibilities

### Analysis

1. Track defence budgets, procurement programmes and competitor moves across Europe and NATO.
2. Build scenarios for the orbital security environment five and ten years out.

### Strategy

1. Translate insights into product strategy and roadmap recommendations.
2. Prepare board and investor briefings that fit on one slide. Two at most.

## Requirements

- Background in strategy consulting, defence policy, or space industry analysis.
- Strong writing; you can explain Kessler syndrome to a general and a CFO.
- Familiarity with EU and NATO space policy.
- Fluent English and German.
