## Mission & responsibilities

You will own the architecture of our propulsion system and make its subsystems work together as one, from requirements through integration to verification.

### System architecture & requirements

1. Own the propulsion system architecture and lead cross-subsystem trade studies
1. Own the system requirements and their flow-down to subsystem specifications
1. Own the preliminary sizing of the main subsystems

### Integration & verification

1. Own system-level integration across the propulsion subsystems (thruster, PCU, fluidics, controller)
1. Maintain the interface-control documentation between subsystems
1. Support verification planning so that subsystem tests roll up into system-level requirements

### Collaboration & content

1. Contribute system-level CAD and technical content for proposals and reporting
1. Be Propulsion's systems-level contact for other departments, e.g. Systems Engineering & AIT

## What we are looking for

### Must have

- 4-8 years of systems-integration experience, ideally on a multi-subsystem hardware product
- Comfortable owning system architecture, requirements, interface documentation and cross-subsystem trade-offs

### Nice to have

- CAD proficiency (mechanical and/or electrical)
- Experience contributing technical content to proposals and technical reports
