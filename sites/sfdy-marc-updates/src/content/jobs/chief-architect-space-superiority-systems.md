## Mission & responsibilities

You will design the technologies and concepts that let Europe hold its ground in a contested space domain, and turn them into systems that decide the outcome.

### Concepts & capability

1. Develop the operational concepts that secure space superiority for Europe and its allies in a contested space domain
1. Translate the needs of the armed forces into capability requirements and a long-term capability roadmap
1. Analyse threats and how the space domain is evolving, and identify where SFDY makes the difference

### Architecture & technologies

1. Design new payload and system concepts that deliver decisive effect in orbit, from blank sheet to architecture
1. Own the system-of-systems architecture that combines our propulsion, platforms, payloads and effectors
1. Work with the Microwave Effectors Lead and the Platforms team to turn concepts into technology and hardware roadmaps

### Validation & partners

1. Validate concepts in simulation together with our Space Domain Simulations team in Vilnius
1. Be SFDY's technical counterpart for the BAAINBw, the Bundeswehr, and European forces and procurement agencies
1. Lead the technical content of studies and proposals for defence customers

## What we are looking for

### Must have

- 10+ years in space or defence systems engineering, architecture or capability development
- A track record of designing new systems or concepts from a blank sheet
- A solid understanding of military operations and how capability requirements are formed, ideally from service or from working alongside the forces
- The standing to argue a concept convincingly in front of senior military and procurement stakeholders
- Fluent English

### Nice to have

- German, a strong plus for work with the BAAINBw and the Bundeswehr
- Experience with space domain awareness, space control or counterspace concepts
- A background in RF, directed energy or lasers, rendezvous and proximity operations, or payload design
- Experience with NATO or EU capability development processes
- Experience with wargaming or simulation-based concept validation
