## Mission & responsibilities

You will sit with our engineers, find the problems that slow them down, and turn them into tools that solve them for good.

### Build tools

1. Work with teams across the organisation to find the hard engineering challenges, and automate business processes where it helps
1. Build the scripts, tools and toolchains that solve them, using AI-assisted development within our toolchain standards
1. Automate engineering processes such as test-data evaluation, reporting and document generation, and, where it helps, business processes in finance, contracts and operations

### Platform & quality

1. Deploy tools on our company platform so the whole team can use them
1. Test each tool against reference cases, and document how it works and where its limits are
1. Maintain and improve tools as needs change

### Enablement

1. Help engineers build tools of their own, and share good practice across the team
1. Work closely with our Chief Architect for Engineering Toolchains in Vilnius

## What we are looking for

### Must have

- 2-5 years of experience, with a degree in engineering, physics or computer science
- Strong Python and scripting skills
- Enough engineering understanding to grasp the problem behind the request
- Experience with AI coding assistants and a disciplined way of using them
- Fluent English

### Nice to have

- German
- Experience with lab or test-bench data, CAD or STEP interfaces
- Experience with containers, continuous integration (CI) or web front-ends
- An interest in space or defence
