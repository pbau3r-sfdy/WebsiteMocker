## Mission & responsibilities

You will give our Propulsion department dedicated, accountable leadership: setting its direction, building its team and delivering on its commitments, side by side with our technical authority for propulsion.

### Department leadership

1. Lead the Propulsion department: day-to-day operations, project management, and change and risk management
1. Own the department's strategic direction and its technology and product-line roadmap
1. Own delivery against all commitments, and set priorities and resources across the department
1. Build the cooperation with neighbouring departments such as Systems Engineering & AIT

### Technical and scientific oversight

1. Act as an independent, critical second opinion on system-level technical decisions, grounded in the state of the art
1. Represent Propulsion in company-level technical reviews and roadmap planning
1. Lead proposal and funding work with ESA, BAAINBw and others

### People & process

1. Build the hiring plan, grow the team, and establish engineering processes, documentation standards and onboarding
1. Own performance management, training and career development
1. Be accountable for personnel safety and security in the department

## What we are looking for

### Must have

- 10-15 years in propulsion or a closely adjacent hardware discipline, including people-leadership experience
- Track record building or scaling a technical team from a small base
- Comfortable operating alongside an existing strong technical authority without needing to own every technical call personally
- Scientific depth to critically assess research and engineering work in the field, typically an advanced degree (MSc or PhD) in aerospace engineering, physics or a related discipline, ideally with a publication record
- Demonstrated experience in operational and project management, including change and risk management and supervising delegated management functions
- Fluent English

### Nice to have

- German, a strong plus for work with BAAINBw
- Experience writing or leading ESA, BAAINBw or comparable funding proposals
- A publication record in propulsion or a related field
