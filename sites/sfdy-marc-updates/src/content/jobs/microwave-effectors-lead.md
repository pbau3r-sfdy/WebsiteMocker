## Mission & responsibilities

You will build the technical foundation of our microwave effector capability from scratch, from first concepts towards an on-orbit payload.

### Technical leadership

1. Build the technical foundation for our microwave effector systems as the founding hire of the function
1. Own delivery of microwave effector-system commitments
1. Own the strategic direction of the function, including the vision for future products, technologies and projects
1. Work closely with our Propulsion team on shared microwave sources

### Technical direction

1. Set the technical architecture and roadmap for microwave effector and payload systems
1. Represent the function in company-level technical reviews
1. Contribute to proposals that fund our activities

### Hiring & team building

1. Define hiring profiles and grow the team
1. Establish engineering processes and documentation standards
1. Be accountable for personnel safety and security in the function

## What we are looking for

### Must have

- 5-10 years of technical leadership in RF/microwave, effector, and/or payload systems, including small-team leadership
- Background in defence- and space-related technical and engineering work
- Comfortable building a technical roadmap from zero and contributing to building a team
- Able to run a close, dotted-line technical relationship with adjacent departments and share common technology bricks

### Nice to have

- Hands-on experience with high-power microwave sources (e.g. magnetron-based systems)
- Experience writing or contributing to ESA, BAAINBw or comparable funding proposals
- Experience developing or qualifying on-orbit payloads
