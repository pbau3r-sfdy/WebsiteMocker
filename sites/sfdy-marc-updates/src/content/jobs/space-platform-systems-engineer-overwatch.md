## Mission & responsibilities

You will take our OVERWATCH demonstrator from design to orbit, and prove in flight what agile, high-endurance space systems can do.

### System design & requirements

1. Own the OVERWATCH platform system design, from mission requirements to architecture and subsystem specifications
1. Own the system budgets (mass, power, propellant, link, pointing) and lead the trade-offs behind them
1. Define the demonstration plan: the systems, modes and manoeuvres OVERWATCH must prove in orbit

### Integration & verification

1. Integrate our own propulsion and payloads with commercial subsystems into one platform, and own the interface-control documentation
1. Own system-level verification and test planning, together with Systems Engineering & AIT
1. Prepare the platform for launch and support early in-orbit operations

### Collaboration

1. Work with Propulsion, Payloads and mission preparation in Vilnius so that platform, payload and mission fit from the start
1. Contribute technical content to reviews, proposals and customer reporting

## What we are looking for

### Must have

- 5-10 years of spacecraft or satellite systems engineering, ideally on hardware that has flown
- Hands-on ownership of requirements, budgets, interfaces and verification on a space platform
- Comfortable setting the system-engineering standards yourself in a small team
- Fluent English

### Nice to have

- German
- Experience with manoeuvring platforms, proximity operations or propulsion integration
- Launch campaign and early-operations (LEOP) experience
- Familiarity with ECSS standards
