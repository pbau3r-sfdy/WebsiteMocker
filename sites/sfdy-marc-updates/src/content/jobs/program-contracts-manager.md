## Mission & responsibilities

You will build the program-management and contract-administration backbone for our publicly funded work, from the first milestone plan to the last invoice.

### Program execution

1. Own the master schedule and milestone plan for publicly funded contracts, including German MOD, DLR and EDF activities
1. Track deliverables against contract obligations and flag schedule or scope risk early
1. Run the recurring program-review cadence across our technical departments

### Contract administration

1. Administer milestone-payment contracts end to end: invoicing triggers, deliverable acceptance and change requests
1. Make sure cross-border work is invoiced correctly
1. Maintain the contract register and keep obligations traceable to the underlying Statement of Work

### Reporting & stakeholder management

1. Prepare progress and financial-status reporting for our funding bodies
1. Be the day-to-day contact for customer program offices between formal review gates

## What we are looking for

### Must have

- 8+ years in program or contract management, ideally in aerospace, defence, or another hardware-heavy, milestone-funded environment
- Working knowledge of German public-contract and milestone-payment structures
- Comfortable owning a schedule and a P&L-adjacent reporting line with no program-management team around them yet
- Fluent German and English, written and spoken

### Nice to have

- Contract experience with German MOD, DLR or EDF-funded programmes
- Experience contributing to and writing proposals
- Understanding of different accounting and cost-calculation methods for public stakeholders
