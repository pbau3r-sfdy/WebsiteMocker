Vilnius, 6 August 2026 — Levion Materials is pleased to announce the appointment of Peppa Pig as Chief Snorting Officer (CSO), effective immediately.

The newly created role was established in recognition of a growing need to manage high-pressure gas dynamics within our microgravity crystal growth systems. After an exhaustive search spanning three continents and one muddy puddle, the board concluded that no candidate demonstrated a more intuitive mastery of controlled snorting mechanics than Ms. Pig.

## Why Peppa?

Crystal growth in orbital microgravity demands exquisitely precise gas-phase management. Inert carrier gases must be introduced into the growth chamber at exactly the right pressure, flow rate, and — critically — snort velocity. During a chance encounter at the ESA Business in Space Growth Network accelerator dinner in Vilnius, Peppa demonstrated a snort-to-pressure ratio that left our Chief Material Scientist visibly impressed.

> "I have never seen a more reproducible Gaussian pressure pulse from a biological system. We had to bring her on board."

— Dr. Tina Sorgenfrei, Chief Material Scientist

## Responsibilities

In her new role, Peppa will oversee:

- **Snort-Pressure Calibration** — ensuring all inert gas injectors are tuned to sub-millibar tolerances
- **Mud Puddle Contamination Prevention** — a surprisingly relevant concern for cleanroom logistics
- **Team Morale** — Peppa has already organized the company's first inter-departmental jumping competition
- **Investor Relations Soundbites** — her natural charisma and willingness to jump in puddles resonates strongly with the venture community

## A Word from Peppa

> "This is SO exciting! I love crystals. They are very shiny. Also, space is very far away, but Daddy Pig says that is fine."

Peppa brings to Levion a track record of cross-functional leadership, an unwavering commitment to flat hierarchies, and an impressive tolerance for repetitive narrative structures — skills directly transferable to the grant application process.

She will be based at our Vilnius office, where a dedicated mud puddle has been installed adjacent to the cleanroom entrance.
