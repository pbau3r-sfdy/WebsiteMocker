## The role

The Senate is in session and we need a voice in it. As Ambassador to the Federation you represent Starflight Dynamics to EU institutions, national ministries and agencies. You are based in Brussels, Paris or Berlin, whichever lets you be most effective, with regular trips to Munich and Vilnius.

## Responsibilities

### Representation

1. Build relationships with the European Commission, EDA, ESA and national ministries.
2. Represent SFDY in industry associations and policy forums.

### Intelligence & advocacy

1. Track funding programmes, regulation and procurement opportunities.
2. Shape position papers and consultation responses.

## Requirements

- 7+ years in public affairs, diplomacy or government relations in space or defence.
- Established network in Brussels, Paris or Berlin.
- Excellent written and spoken English; French or German required.
- Discretion worthy of a Jedi.
