## The role

Orbit is crowded with spent stages, fragments and the occasional lost glove. You will build our space situational awareness capability: tracking debris, assessing collision risks and informing how our vehicles manoeuvre and operate.

## Responsibilities

### Awareness

1. Ingest and analyse tracking data from public and commercial sources.
2. Compute conjunction risks and recommend avoidance manoeuvres.

### Capability

1. Develop tools for debris characterisation and orbit prediction.
2. Work with partners on future active debris removal concepts.

## Requirements

- Degree in astrodynamics, aerospace engineering or physics.
- Experience with orbit determination tools (e.g. GMAT, Orekit, STK).
- Python proficiency.
- Fluent English.
