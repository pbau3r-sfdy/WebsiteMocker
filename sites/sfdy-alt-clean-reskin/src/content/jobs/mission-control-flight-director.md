## The role

When the call is "go / no-go", you make it. The Flight Director leads mission operations from our Munich control room, builds the operations team and procedures, and keeps everyone calm when telemetry turns red.

## Responsibilities

### Operations

1. Lead mission rehearsals and live operations.
2. Own go / no-go decisions and the flight rules behind them.

### Team & procedures

1. Build and train the flight control team.
2. Write and maintain operations procedures and contingency plans.

## Requirements

- 7+ years in spacecraft or launch operations.
- Experience leading a control room team.
- Unshakeable calm.
- Fluent English; German is a plus.
