## The role

Kessel Run or not, parts have to arrive on time. You own procurement, inventory and shipping for our Munich site, including export-controlled goods and international shipments to Vilnius and partners.

## Responsibilities

### Supply chain

1. Manage purchasing, suppliers and delivery schedules.
2. Run inventory and stores for lab and workshop.

### Compliance

1. Handle export-control paperwork and customs.
2. Keep shipping records audit-ready.

## Requirements

- Training or experience in logistics, procurement or supply chain.
- Experience with export control (BAFA) is a strong plus.
- Organised and persistent.
- Fluent German and English.
