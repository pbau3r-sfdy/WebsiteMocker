## The role

That is no moon. Well, this time it is. You will develop concepts for long-term lunar and cislunar infrastructure that our materials and propulsion work could enable, and build partnerships to make them credible.

## Responsibilities

### Concepts

1. Develop concept studies for lunar and cislunar infrastructure.
2. Assess technology readiness and the critical path.

### Partnerships

1. Engage with agencies, universities and industry partners.
2. Support proposals for ESA and national programmes.

## Requirements

- Background in space architecture, systems engineering or aerospace.
- Experience with concept studies or ESA projects.
- Strong visual communication.
- Fluent English.
