## The role

Stay on target. As an X-Wing Pilot you will fly mission rehearsals in our simulator, support vehicle operations from the control room and give engineering direct feedback on how our spacecraft handle when things get tight. Trench runs are rare. Checklists are not.

## Responsibilities

### Flight

1. Fly simulated mission profiles, from launch window to deorbit.
2. Run pre-flight checklists with an R2 unit and, increasingly, with our flight software.
3. Debrief every sortie and file anomaly reports within 24 hours.

### Engineering feedback

1. Work with the GNC team on handling qualities and manual override modes.
2. Test cockpit interfaces and tell us honestly which buttons should not be next to each other.

## Requirements

- Flight experience: aircraft, drones, simulators or a well-documented history of bullseyeing womp rats.
- Calm radio discipline. "Woohoo!" is permitted once per mission.
- Background in aerospace engineering or physics is a plus.
- Fluent English.
