## The role

Kyber crystals are hard to find. Pure advanced materials manufactured in microgravity are harder. At our Vilnius site you will develop and characterise crystalline and composite materials and prepare experiments for our first in-orbit demonstrators.

## Responsibilities

### Research

1. Develop synthesis and growth processes for advanced crystalline materials.
2. Characterise samples (XRD, SEM, spectroscopy) with partner labs in Lithuania.

### Flight experiments

1. Design payload experiments for microgravity manufacturing.
2. Publish results, or classify them, as appropriate.

## Requirements

- PhD or MSc in materials science, chemistry or physics.
- Hands-on lab experience with crystal growth or thin films.
- Curiosity about microgravity manufacturing.
- Fluent English; Lithuanian is a plus.
