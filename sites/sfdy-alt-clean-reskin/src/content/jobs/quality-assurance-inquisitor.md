## The role

Every part needs a pedigree and every test needs a record. You build our quality management system towards ISO 9001 and EN 9100, run inspections and audits, and make sure nothing flies that should not.

## Responsibilities

### Quality system

1. Build and maintain the QMS towards ISO 9001 and EN 9100.
2. Run internal audits and supplier audits.

### Product assurance

1. Lead nonconformance reviews and root-cause analyses.
2. Own incoming inspection and configuration control.

## Requirements

- Degree in engineering or equivalent experience.
- 3+ years in quality assurance for aerospace or defence.
- Auditor qualification is a plus.
- Fluent German and English.
