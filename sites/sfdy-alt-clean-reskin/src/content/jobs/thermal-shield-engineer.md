## The role

Re-entry is hot. Mustafar-hot. You will develop and test thermal protection materials and coatings at our Vilnius site, from lab samples to flight-representative panels.

## Responsibilities

### Materials

1. Develop ablative and reusable thermal protection materials.
2. Characterise thermal and mechanical properties.

### Testing

1. Plan arc-jet and thermal vacuum test campaigns with partners.
2. Feed results into thermal models.

## Requirements

- Degree in materials science or mechanical engineering.
- Experience with composites, ceramics or coatings.
- Thermal analysis skills are a plus.
- Fluent English.
