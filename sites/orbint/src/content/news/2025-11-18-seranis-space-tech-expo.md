Leonhard Kessler and Daniel Weinzierl from the Universität der Bundeswehr München presented the SeRANIS mission and the university spin-off Orbint at a joint booth during Space Tech Expo Europe in Bremen.

The presentation offered the professional community a detailed look into next-generation satellite technology development, covering both the research mission that gave rise to Orbint and the commercial signal intelligence capabilities the company is building.

SeRANIS (Seamless Radio Access Networks for Internet of Space) operates under the German Armed Forces Center for Digitalization and Technology Research (dtec.bw), which receives funding through the European Union's NextGenerationEU initiative. The SPACE Research Center at UniBw M carries out the project.
