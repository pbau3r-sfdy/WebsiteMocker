At ILA Berlin 2026, Europe's premier aerospace and defense exhibition, Orbint announced a strategic partnership with Airbus and Rohde & Schwarz — a significant step in building a sovereign European space-based signal intelligence capability.

The partnership brings together Orbint's distributed satellite network technology and onboard signal processing expertise with Airbus's satellite integration capabilities and Rohde & Schwarz's deep electromagnetic spectrum expertise.

The collaboration aims to accelerate the development of a full-spectrum signal intelligence system that can be operated in sovereign European hands — serving both national defense requirements and allied security frameworks.

ILA Berlin 2026 drew over 1,000 exhibitors and tens of thousands of attendees from across the global aerospace industry, providing a prominent stage for the announcement.
