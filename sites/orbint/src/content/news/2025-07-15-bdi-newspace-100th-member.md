Orbint GmbH has joined the BDI NewSpace Initiative, becoming its 100th member company — a milestone for Germany's emerging commercial space ecosystem.

As a spin-off from the dtec.bw-supported SeRANIS project at Munich's Bundeswehr University, Orbint specializes in space-based signal intelligence through a distributed satellite network capable of detecting, identifying, and precisely locating electromagnetic signals in near real-time.

The company enables customers to obtain reliable, current situational awareness and actionable intelligence — supporting security and strategic decision-making at a global scale.

The NewSpace Initiative welcomed founding team members Alexander Eckert, Simon Heine, Daniel Weinzierl, and Winfried Stock, along with the broader Orbint team.

*Event held at Berlin Brandenburg International Airport.*
