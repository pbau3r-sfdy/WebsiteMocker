At Berlin's *Tag der Raumfahrt* (Space Day), Orbint co-founder Alexander Eckert sat down with SpaceWatch.Global TV for a Space Café interview, discussing the company's vision for space-based signal intelligence.

Eckert emphasized that Orbint aims to revolutionize global signal detection and analysis through advanced satellite capabilities. The team draws on deep expertise in signal intelligence and satellite systems engineering, built at the University of the Bundeswehr Munich.

The conversation positioned satellite technology as increasingly critical for both communications and security applications — and framed Orbint's distributed approach as potentially transformative for the intelligence sector.
