Technology company Rohde & Schwarz has acquired a stake in Orbint GmbH, a spin-off from Germany's Bundeswehr University Munich. The two firms will collaborate to strengthen Europe's technological sovereignty and security in space through satellite-based signal intelligence capabilities.

Through this investment, Rohde & Schwarz expands its electromagnetic spectrum reconnaissance portfolio into the space domain. Orbint was founded in 2025 by four researchers from the Bundeswehr University: Alexander Eckert, Simon Heine, Daniel Weinzierl, and Winfried Stock. The startup develops distributed satellite networks capable of detecting, identifying, and localizing signals in near-real time, with onboard data processing.

## Key Statements

Alexander Orellano, Executive Vice President at Rohde & Schwarz, noted that "space-based signal intelligence represents a crucial capability for Europe" and praised Orbint's specialized expertise.

Alexander Philipp, managing director of Rohde & Schwarz's sales division, emphasized that the partnership enables offering "a sovereign solution with national capacities" to the German armed forces.

Co-founder Alexander Eckert stated the team selected "a partner who brings not only capital but genuine added value through technological depth."

## Research to Implementation

Orbint emerged from the SeRANIS small satellite mission, supported by dtec.bw, which provides an orbital experimental laboratory for space-based communications and reconnaissance technologies.

University president Eva-Maria Kern highlighted how the spin-off demonstrates the institution's commitment to "security and defense-relevant research."
