The Orbint team joined Rohde & Schwarz at the International Distributor Conference (IDC) 2026, presenting the company's satellite-based signal intelligence capabilities to an international audience of defense and security technology professionals.

The presentation highlighted Orbint's distributed satellite architecture and its applications across electromagnetic order of battle mapping, maritime surveillance, and crisis early warning — showcasing how onboard processing enables near-real-time intelligence delivery.

The partnership between Orbint and Rohde & Schwarz, announced in November 2025, positions the two organizations to jointly address European sovereign requirements for space-based SIGINT.
