## The role

We are growing fast and someone needs to know where the onboarding checklist is. As Protocol Droid Wrangler you run onboarding, coordinate office life in Munich and keep our people processes running smoothly, without ever saying "the odds of successfully navigating HR are 3,720 to 1".

## Responsibilities

### People operations

1. Run onboarding and offboarding for both sites.
2. Maintain personnel files, contracts paperwork and the holiday calendar.

### Office & culture

1. Organise team events, from lab barbecues to launch-viewing nights.
2. Keep the Munich office stocked, tidy and droid-friendly.

## Requirements

- Experience in HR administration, office management or similar.
- Organised, discreet and friendly.
- Fluent German and English.
- Willingness to work 20–25 hours per week.
