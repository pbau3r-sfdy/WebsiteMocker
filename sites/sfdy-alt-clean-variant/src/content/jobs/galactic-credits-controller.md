## The role

The Empire built two Death Stars without a budget review. We will not make that mistake. You own accounting, controlling and grant reporting for the GmbH and support the Lithuanian entity, keeping investors, auditors and funding bodies happy.

## Responsibilities

### Controlling

1. Run monthly closing, budgets and forecasts.
2. Build cost reporting per programme and department.

### Funding

1. Prepare grant and contract reporting for ESA, BAAINBw and EU programmes.
2. Support fundraising data rooms and investor reporting.

## Requirements

- Degree in business, finance or accounting.
- 4+ years in controlling, ideally in a start-up or defence company.
- Experience with public funding reporting is a strong plus.
- Fluent German and English.
