## The role

"It's not my fault!" is not something you will need to say. As Hyperdrive Technician you assemble, integrate and maintain propulsion test hardware, keep the test stand running and are the first to know when something sounds wrong.

## Responsibilities

### Hardware

1. Assemble and integrate propulsion units, feed systems and harnesses.
2. Perform inspections, leak checks and preventive maintenance.

### Test stand

1. Prepare and support hot-fire and cold-flow test campaigns.
2. Keep the workshop tidy enough that a Wookiee can find the hydrospanner.

## Requirements

- Completed technical training (e.g. Mechatroniker, Industriemechaniker) or equivalent.
- Experience with high-pressure systems, cleanliness standards or aerospace hardware.
- Hands-on, careful and allergic to undocumented changes.
- German or English; both is ideal.
