## The role

Our best holograms deserve better than a blurry message stored in a droid. You run our communications: news, social channels, press relations and the occasional launch-day livestream.

## Responsibilities

### Content

1. Write news posts, social updates and newsletters.
2. Produce photo and video content with the engineering teams.

### Press

1. Build relationships with space, defence and tech journalists.
2. Coordinate announcements with partners and customers.

## Requirements

- 3+ years in communications, PR or content marketing.
- Excellent writing in English; German or Lithuanian is a plus.
- Comfortable with technical topics.
- Available 20–30 hours per week.
