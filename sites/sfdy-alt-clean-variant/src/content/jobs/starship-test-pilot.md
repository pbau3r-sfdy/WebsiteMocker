## The role

Some people fly what others built. Test pilots help build what others will fly. You will define test points, fly simulated and hardware-in-the-loop campaigns and bring the operator's voice into vehicle design.

## Responsibilities

### Test

1. Define and fly test cards for new vehicle capabilities.
2. Analyse results with engineering and recommend changes.

### Design input

1. Contribute to cockpit, HMI and autonomy design.
2. Represent operators in design reviews.

## Requirements

- Test pilot school graduate or extensive flight-test experience.
- Engineering degree.
- Clear, factual reporting style.
- Fluent English.
