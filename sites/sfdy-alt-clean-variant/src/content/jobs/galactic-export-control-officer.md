## The role

These are not the droids you are looking for. Unless they are on the dual-use list. You own export control and trade compliance across both sites: classifications, licences and training.

## Responsibilities

### Compliance

1. Classify products and technology under EU and German export rules.
2. Prepare licence applications with BAFA.

### Programme

1. Train staff on export control and sanctions.
2. Run internal audits and keep the compliance programme current.

## Requirements

- Experience in export control for defence or dual-use goods.
- Knowledge of EU Dual-Use Regulation and AWG/AWV.
- Meticulous and pragmatic.
- Fluent German and English.
