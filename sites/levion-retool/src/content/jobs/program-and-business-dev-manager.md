## Mission & responsibilities

As Program & Business Development Manager for Microgravity Materials, you will drive the institutional and programmatic backbone of our in-space materials roadmap. Your responsibilities include:

### Programme delivery

1. **Executing the materials roadmap** - coordinating the steps that ultimately lead to the first commercially viable in-space–produced materials.
1. **Coordinating with supporting agencies** - serving as the primary interface to ESA, DLR, EIC, and relevant national or regional partners.
1. **Securing and delivering institutional programmes** - leading bid preparation and execution for ESA CASE, ESA BSGN, EIC, and related funding instruments.
1. **Acquiring further programmes** - identifying and shaping additional institutional opportunities as the materials and payloads track expands.
1. **Maintaining programme structure** - ensuring documentation, reporting, timelines, and deliverables are consistently met across all materials-related activities.
1. **Coordinating partners** - supporting collaboration with research institutions, microgravity providers, and ecosystem stakeholders to ensure smooth execution.

### Business development

1. **Shaping the business side of the programme:** define and refine the business model, identify target markets and use cases, and secure early adopters and pilot customers (e.g. via LOIs/MOUs)

This is a role for someone who brings clarity, structure, and ownership to a multi-year institutional roadmap - and who can keep agency programmes, partners, and internal teams aligned as the materials track scales.

## What we are looking for

### Required

- Experience in programme or project management for complex R&D or technology programmes.
- Proven track record working with institutional funding bodies (e.g., ESA, DLR, EU/EIC, national agencies) — either running programmes or preparing bids, ideally both.
- Strong capability in documentation, reporting, timelines, and deliverables — able to run institutional projects end-to-end with discipline.
- Experience coordinating multi-party technical ecosystems (research institutes, suppliers, agencies, industry partners).
- High clarity and ownership — able to structure ambiguity, drive progress, and keep stakeholders aligned.
- Fluent English, Fluent German; excellent written communication

## What we offer

- A high-ownership role shaping Europe’s in-space materials capability.
- Direct responsibility for flagship ESA, DLR, and EU programs.
- Autonomy in a fast-moving, deeptech environment.
- Close collaboration with leading European partners (ESA, DLR, FTMC).
- Flexible hybrid setup; periodic travel in Europe.
- Competitive compensation.

## Interview process

The interviewing process will consist of three stages:

- **Screening phase:** If your application is interesting to us, we will reach out for an initial interview, to get to know you, get a brief understanding on your experience, and clarify administrative boundary conditions. You can ask any questions at this stage as well.
- **Interview stage:** In this stage, we will send you a small task relevant to the position you applied for. Typically, we will hold one interview, which will cover the presentation of your task’s results.
- **Contract stage:** We will provide you with an offer and will of course provide you with the opportunity to discuss it and ask questions.
