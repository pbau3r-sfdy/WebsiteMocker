## Mission & responsibilities

You will be SFDY's first strategist for the High Frontier: shaping how Europe can prevail in space, and opening the doors that turn our ideas into demonstrators and programmes.

### Theory of victory & doctrine

1. Develop SFDY's theory of victory in space and on its fringes, from Earth orbit to cislunar space, for nations acting alone or together
1. Turn the CEO's direction into a capability roadmap with our teams: what to build, what to demonstrate, and where to invest next
1. Watch the field, the threats and what physics and technology permit, and keep the roadmap ahead of them

### Forces & partners

1. Be SFDY's counterpart to military planners, capability developers and space commands in Germany, NATO and allied nations
1. Place SFDY's concepts, tools and people in the working groups where doctrine and requirements take shape
1. Bring our simulation tools to the forces as the place to test concepts and prepare for operations

### Business & demonstrators

1. Open the doors that lead to business, and steer our business development efforts through them
1. Get demonstrators started with defence customers, and position SFDY in German and European defence space programmes
1. Represent SFDY with authority before senior decision-makers, at conferences and in publications

## What we are looking for

### Must have

- 15+ years in defence, ideally as an officer with experience in operational planning, capability development or doctrine, in space or a closely related domain
- Credibility with military planners and senior decision-makers, and the ability to make complex technology understandable
- Strategic acumen: you can read a theory of war for a domain that is still being defined, and build on it
- Enough technical depth to judge what is possible in orbit, from platforms to electronic warfare, directed energy and lasers
- Fluent German and English

### Nice to have

- French, a plus for work with French and other European space forces
- Experience with the Bundeswehr Space Command, NATO or another European space command
- A network in German and European defence planning and capability development
- Experience with wargaming, operational analysis or concept development and experimentation
