## Mission & responsibilities

You will build SFDY's IT and information-security foundation from the ground up, running the infrastructure and setting the policy for a defence-adjacent company.

### IT infrastructure

1. Own day-to-day IT infrastructure (devices, network, cloud and on-premises systems) for our growing Munich team and our remote team
1. Select and roll out core IT tooling as headcount grows

### Information security & compliance

1. Own information-security policy and practice for a defence-adjacent company handling sensitive programme data
1. Support controlled-information handling procedures and access controls
1. Prepare the company for government-mandated security certification or audit

### Onboarding & support

1. Own IT onboarding and offboarding for new hires
1. Provide day-to-day technical support to the team

## What we are looking for

### Must have

- 7+ years in IT infrastructure and/or information security, ideally with some exposure to classified or controlled-information environments (government, defence, or another regulated industry)
- Comfortable being the company's first dedicated IT/security hire, setting policy as well as running infrastructure
- Fluent English

### Nice to have

- German
- Experience preparing an organisation for a security certification or audit
