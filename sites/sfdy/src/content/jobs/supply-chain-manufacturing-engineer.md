## Mission & responsibilities

You will get the right parts, from the right suppliers, to our benches on time, and make sure what we design can actually be built.

### Sourcing & suppliers

1. Find, assess and qualify suppliers for pieces, parts and assemblies, from machined parts to flight-grade subsystems
1. Run requests for quotation, compare offers, and give engineers solid input for make-or-buy decisions
1. Build and maintain our supplier base and its qualification records

### Orders & delivery

1. Own purchasing from order to delivery: purchase orders, lead times, expediting and incoming inspection
1. Keep procurement traceable, with certificates of conformity, material records and documentation that meet space quality standards
1. Handle export-control and dual-use requirements for what we buy and where it comes from

### Design for manufacturing

1. Work with Propulsion, Platforms and Payloads so that parts can be built, bought and scaled
1. Support the step from prototypes to repeatable production for our platforms

## What we are looking for

### Must have

- 3-6 years in procurement, supply chain or manufacturing engineering, ideally for space-grade or other high-reliability hardware
- A solid grasp of mechanical manufacturing: machining, additive manufacturing, surface treatment, and how they drive cost and lead time
- Familiarity with space quality and documentation standards (e.g. ECSS)
- A working knowledge of export-control and dual-use rules (EU, BAFA, ITAR/EAR)
- A basic understanding of purchasing contracts and terms
- Fluent German and English

### Nice to have

- Experience with flight-hardware procurement or supplier audits
- Experience setting up procurement processes and ERP tooling in a growing company
- A technical degree in mechanical, aerospace or production engineering
