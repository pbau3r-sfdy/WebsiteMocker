Weßling, 06 August 2024 – We are delighted to announce that Carolina Rocha, a first-class entrepreneur, and experienced manager will be joining the SFDY crew from September on!

> "With her background, business experience and skills, Carolina will be an invaluable asset to the future of SFDY!"

— Dr. Philipp Bauer, CEO of STARFLIGHT DYNAMICS

## About Carolina Rocha:

With over nine years of working on her own startups and at P&G, Carolina has built an impressive track record of driving business growth and fostering strategic relationships.

Her skills range from securing investments and executing M&A deals to developing effective business strategies and beyond. Carolina's experience in ambiguous, fast-paced environments has made her a seasoned entrepreneur and a high-performing, results-oriented leader dedicated to transforming business opportunities into long-term growth.

**With the addition of Carolina, we are now an even stronger and more capable team, united in our pursuit of bringing industry to space.**

Welcome to SFDY, Carolina!
