We're thrilled to share that Starflight Dynamics has been accepted into the prestigious Creative Destruction Lab (CDL) Space Stream!

This marks a major milestone for our journey toward revolutionizing industry in space. We're confident that CDL's mentorship and support will help accelerate our mission to take manufacturing beyond Earth's gravity. The first session kicks off this November in Toronto, and we can't wait to get started!

## What is the Creative Destruction Lab?

CDL is a global program designed to help seed-stage, science-based companies scale. It connects startups with a distinguished network of entrepreneurs, angel investors, economists, and scientists through an objectives-driven mentoring process. We're honored to be part of such a transformative initiative!

Learn more about CDL: [Creative Destruction Lab](https://creativedestructionlab.com/)
