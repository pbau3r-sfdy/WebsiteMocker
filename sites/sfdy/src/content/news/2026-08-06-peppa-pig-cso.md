Weßling, 6 August 2026 — STARFLIGHT DYNAMICS is proud to announce the appointment of Peppa Pig as Chief Snorting Officer (CSO), effective immediately.

## A New Kind of Crew Member

Space travel has always attracted the bold, the brilliant, and the slightly unhinged. Today, STARFLIGHT DYNAMICS adds a new archetype to that list: the profoundly snorty.

After a global search, we are proud to confirm that Peppa Pig has accepted the role of Chief Snorting Officer — a strategic position created to leverage her unique expertise in high-velocity exhalation dynamics, atmospheric boundary layer analysis, and mud puddle fluid mechanics.

## Mission Fit

Our propulsion team has long known that the key bottleneck in upper-stage engine ignition is not thrust — it is snort timing. Peppa's biometric snort data, collected over a distinguished career of jumping in puddles, shows a mean impulse profile that maps almost perfectly onto our LOX/methane igniter pulse sequence.

> "We ran the numbers. Twice. Then we ran them again just to be sure. Peppa's nasal output at sea level is statistically indistinguishable from our optimal igniter pre-charge waveform. We had no choice but to hire her."

— Chief Propulsion Engineer, SFDY

## Duties at SFDY

- Pre-launch snort protocol (replacing legacy compressed air systems)
- Weekly all-hands morale broadcasts featuring puddle updates
- Lead narrator for SFDY's new investor explainer video series
