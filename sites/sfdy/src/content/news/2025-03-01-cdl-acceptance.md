CDL connects us with an exceptional network of serial entrepreneurs, leading scientists, and investors who understand what it takes to build transformative technology companies. We are looking forward to the sessions ahead and the perspective they bring.

This is another building block in our strategy to surround ourselves with the best people in hard tech as we scale.
