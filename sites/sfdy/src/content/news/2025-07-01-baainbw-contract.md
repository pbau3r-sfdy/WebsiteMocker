We are proud to announce that Starflight Dynamics has been awarded a contract by BAAINBw — the Federal Office of Bundeswehr Equipment, Information Technology and In-Service Support.

This contract marks a significant milestone in our mission to develop high-mobility orbital systems for sovereign applications. Working with one of Europe's largest defence procurement organisations validates both our technology and our team's readiness to deliver at programme scale.

We look forward to sharing more details as the programme progresses.
