After a period of strategic reflection, Starflight Dynamics has made the decision to sharpen its focus on high-mobility orbital systems and sovereign applications.

As part of this refocus, we have spun off our advanced materials division into a new independent entity: **Levion Materials**. The team, IP, and operational infrastructure for advanced materials will continue under the Levion banner, with Starflight Dynamics maintaining a collaborative relationship with the new company.

We are proud of what the materials team built and confident that Levion is well-positioned to take that work forward. For Starflight Dynamics, this move allows us to concentrate fully on our orbital systems mission.

We wish the Levion Materials team every success.
