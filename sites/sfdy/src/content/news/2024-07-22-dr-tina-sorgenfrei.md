Weßling, 22 July 2024 – We are thrilled to welcome Dr. Tina Sorgenfrei to the SFDY crew!

## About Dr. Tina Sorgenfrei:

She is the leading crystallography expert for zero gravity epitaxy in Germany. A former researcher at the Institute of Earth and Environmental Sciences at the University of Freiburg, with over 20 peer-reviewed publications, Tina is a true expert in crystallography and in-space material science, especially in the field of semiconductors.

> "Tina will be a key player in setting up our in-space production activities. We're excited to reach for the stars together! Ad Astra!"

— Dr. Philipp Bauer, CEO of STARFLIGHT DYNAMICS
