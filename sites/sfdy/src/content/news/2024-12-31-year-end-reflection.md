As 2024 draws to a close, we want to take a moment to reflect on the year and what it has meant for Starflight Dynamics.

It has been a year of building — of assembling the team, validating key technical approaches, and laying the operational foundations we need to grow. We have brought on exceptional people, started important partnerships, and taken our first steps into the defence and sovereign applications space.

2025 will be the year we execute. We are grateful to everyone who has supported us — investors, partners, team members, and the broader community who believes in what we are building.
