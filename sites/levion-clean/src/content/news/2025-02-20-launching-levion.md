Levion Materials grew out of the Advanced Materials division of Starflight Dynamics GmbH
and is currently under incorporation as an independent company.

## Why separate the company

Demand has accelerated across both core business areas, with strong pull from their
respective markets, with distinct customer needs, partner ecosystems, and success
factors. Advanced materials and space systems are best served by two focused
organisations rather than one divided one.

## What Levion does

Crystal growth as-a-service: customers send semiconductor feedstock, Levion grows the
ingot in orbit and returns it. The customer's downstream process does not change.
