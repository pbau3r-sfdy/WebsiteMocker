We are proud to announce that Starflight Dynamics has been awarded a research contract by the German Federal Office of Bundeswehr Equipment, Information Technology and In-Service Support (BAAINBw) through the Military Technical Center 61 in Manching to develop a new generation of reactive, green orbital propulsion technologies.

**The project is titled ReaGAn – Reactive Green Propulsion Technology.**

## DETAILS & INSIGHTS:

Under ReaGAn, we will conduct feasibility studies, develop test infrastructure, and perform early-stage system evaluations to explore new approaches for operating agile orbital platforms.

The initiative focuses on orbital propulsion systems optimized for speed, endurance, and deployment flexibility – enabling future missions where space-based assets serve not only as passive sensors but as responsive capabilities.

## FOR EUROPE IN SPACE:

The technology unlocks new capabilities for the Bundeswehr and its partners: extended endurance and mobility in orbit, rapid and precise satellite deployment, and sustainable, dual-use propulsion solutions for security-relevant missions.

ReaGAn thus directly addresses key needs in space situational awareness, operational responsiveness, and European technological sovereignty in orbit.

— Dr. Philipp Bauer, founder and CEO of SFDY.
