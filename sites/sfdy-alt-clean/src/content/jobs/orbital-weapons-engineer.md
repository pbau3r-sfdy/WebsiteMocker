## The role

Every superweapon in history was undone by one small design flaw. Your job is to make sure ours does not have one. You will design, analyse and test defensive payload subsystems for our orbital vehicles, working closely with Propulsion and Systems Engineering under strict export-control and security rules.

## Responsibilities

### Design

1. Develop concepts and detailed designs for defensive payload subsystems.
2. Run trade studies on mass, power and thermal budgets.
3. Perform a formal exhaust-port review on every design. We mean every design.

### Verification

1. Define test campaigns and support qualification on the ground.
2. Document everything to defence-programme standards.

## Requirements

- MSc in aerospace, mechanical or electrical engineering.
- 5+ years in defence or space systems engineering.
- Experience with classified or export-controlled programmes.
- Fluent English; German is a strong plus.
