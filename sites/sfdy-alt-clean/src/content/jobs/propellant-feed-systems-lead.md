## The role

Every great engine depends on its plumbing. You lead the design and qualification of propellant feed systems, valves and tanks, and grow a small team around them.

## Responsibilities

### Technical lead

1. Own feed system architecture, from tank to injector.
2. Lead component selection and supplier qualification.

### Team

1. Mentor engineers and technicians.
2. Plan test campaigns with the test-stand crew.

## Requirements

- 7+ years in fluid systems for space or launch.
- Experience with high-pressure and cryogenic systems.
- Leadership experience.
- Fluent English; German is a plus.
