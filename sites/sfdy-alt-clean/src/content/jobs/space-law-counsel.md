## The role

Treaties, contracts and the occasional Galactic Senate inquiry. As our first in-house lawyer you handle commercial contracts, defence procurement terms, IP and the emerging field of space law.

## Responsibilities

### Contracts

1. Draft and negotiate customer, supplier and partner agreements.
2. Advise on public procurement and defence contract terms.

### Governance

1. Manage IP, NDAs and corporate housekeeping for both entities.
2. Track space law and regulation developments in the EU.

## Requirements

- Fully qualified lawyer (Volljurist) or equivalent.
- Experience in commercial, procurement or aerospace law.
- Pragmatic: you find ways to say yes safely.
- Fluent German and English.
