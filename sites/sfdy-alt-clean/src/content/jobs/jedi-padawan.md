## The role

Every great Jedi began by sweeping the temple floor. At Starflight Dynamics you will begin by sweeping the cleanroom floor, but with the Force, which is considerably faster and leaves no lint. As a Padawan you are paired with a Master who will guide your training across advanced materials, payload integration and the ancient art of reading a test report without skipping to the conclusions.

## Responsibilities

### Training

1. Complete daily lightsaber forms, safely and strictly outside the cleanroom.
2. Practise levitating components up to 5 kg. Larger items require a Master and a filled-in lifting plan.
3. Meditate on failure modes before every test campaign, then write them down in the FMEA like everyone else.

### Mission support

1. Support the materials team with sample preparation, handling and delicate Force-assisted alignment.
2. Accompany your Master on missions to partner labs, carrying the coffee and asking excellent questions.
3. Maintain the holocron, also known as the shared drive, in a state your Master can actually navigate.

### Personal growth

1. Keep a training journal and present one lesson learned per sprint review.
2. Resist the temptation to finish your training early. You are not ready. Nobody ever is.

## Requirements

- A strong interest in materials science, physics or engineering; a degree in progress or completed.
- Patience. A lot of it. More than that.
- The humility to accept that "do or do not, there is no try" also applies to documentation.
- Comfortable with English; basic Lithuanian, Shyriiwook or Binary is a plus.
- Your own robe. We supply the lab coat.
