## Phase Goal

**As a** site operator, **I want to** have brand hashtags suggested and off-brand terms flagged whenever I write content, **so that** my sites maintain consistent voice without manual lookup of brand guidelines.

<objective>
Insert a brand signal check step into all four content skills. The check is silent when no brand block exists (or all arrays are empty) and active when brand data is present. It covers hashtag suggestions, bi-directional enrichment (opt-in), avoid-word scanning (non-blocking), and vocabulary nudges. wm-add-job gets a narrower treatment (no hashtag enrichment). All four skills also get an explicit hashtag source priority rule: wiring.json brand.hashtags first, keywords.json as fallback.

Purpose: Every content write through these skills becomes brand-aware automatically — operators get suggestions without setup friction, and sites with no brand data are completely unaffected.

Output: Four modified skill files in _core/.claude/skills/, each with a "Brand signal check" step inserted at the correct position before the commit step.
</objective>
