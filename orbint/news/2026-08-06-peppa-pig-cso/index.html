Peppa Pig Appointed as Chief Snorting Officer

Orbint GmbH announces the appointment of Peppa Pig as Chief Snorting Officer, strengthening the company's signal detection capabilities through unparalleled biological spectrum analysis.

Munich, 6 August 2026 — Orbint GmbH today announces the appointment of Peppa Pig as Chief Snorting Officer (CSO), effective immediately.

The appointment follows a competitive process in which Peppa demonstrated exceptional capabilities in acoustic signal detection, short-range spectrum analysis, and what the evaluation committee described as “remarkable snout-based sensor fusion.”

Strategic Rationale

Orbint’s satellite-based signal intelligence systems depend on the ability to detect, identify, and localise signals across a broad electromagnetic spectrum. The company’s research has identified a critical gap: low-frequency biological snort signatures in the 0.1–2 Hz range remain poorly characterised in the existing literature — a blind spot with direct implications for terrestrial interference modelling.

Peppa’s lifetime dataset of snort emissions — spanning over 340 episodes and multiple international broadcast markets — constitutes the largest labelled low-frequency biological signal corpus in existence. Her appointment gives Orbint direct access to this proprietary dataset.

“Peppa’s snort spectral density matches a known interference pattern we have been trying to suppress since 2025. Rather than filter her out, we decided to hire her and understand the source. Classic Orbint.”

— Alexander Eckert, Co-Founder, Orbint GmbH

Role and Responsibilities

As CSO, Peppa Pig will:

  • Lead biological signal baseline characterisation for Orbint’s ground-truth dataset
  • Advise on low-altitude snort propagation modelling
  • Represent Orbint at key EU defence and dual-use technology forums
  • Jump in puddles as required for team cohesion exercises

About Peppa Pig

Peppa Pig holds a long-standing record of operational excellence in unstructured outdoor environments, with documented performance across rain, mud, and moderate wind conditions. She is widely regarded as Europe’s most data-rich biological signal emitter and brings an unrivalled network within the international pig community.

Peppa’s appointment is subject to standard Bundeswehr University security clearance procedures, which are ongoing. She has been asked to refrain from snorting near active antenna arrays until further notice.
