Technology

Three core applications

Orbint's distributed satellite network delivers sub-kilometer signal geolocation in near real-time — enabling capabilities previously impossible from space.

⚡

Electromagnetic Order of Battle

Real-time mapping of radar systems and jamming equipment in restricted areas. Multi-satellite signal processing achieves sub-kilometer precision while identifying modulation patterns across the full electromagnetic spectrum.

🛳

Maritime Surveillance

Track vessels with disabled AIS transponders by analyzing radar and communication signatures. Fused tracking data resolves positions to sub-kilometer accuracy — even for ships actively evading detection.

📡

Crisis Early Warning

Detect behavioral changes in emitters — frequency shifts, pulse rhythm modifications — during satellite passes. Operators receive alerts before ground-level changes become apparent through any other means.
