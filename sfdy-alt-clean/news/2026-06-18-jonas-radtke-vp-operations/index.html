New at SFDY: Jonas Radtke, VP Operations

Jonas Radtke officially welcomed as VP Operations. His experience, sharp mind and operational expertise help us raise the bar across many areas of our next phase of growth.

Munich, 18 June 2026 – We are excited to officially welcome Jonas Radtke as VP Operations at SFDY!

Jonas originally joined us in autumn 2025 and has been fully on board since May 2026. Since joining, he has made a substantial impact across SFDY.

His experience, sharp mind and operational expertise help us raise the bar across many of the areas that matter most for our next phase of growth.

He combines strategic thinking with hands-on execution in a way that elevates both the quality and pace of our overall operations – helping us improve, scale and move faster across key areas of the business.

“With his exceptional expertise and deep understanding of the space industry, Jonas delivers enormous value to SFDY. He was the missing piece – the person who helps us become stronger as an organization, more effective as a company, and even more capable as a SpaceTech provider.”

— Dr. Philipp Bauer, CEO of STARFLIGHT DYNAMICS

About Jonas:

As co-founder of OKAPI:Orbits, Jonas has spent 7+ years shaping the company’s strategy and growth in Space Traffic Management, helping to bring it from an idea into an established NewSpace player. He has led major technology and funding projects with organizations such as DLR, ESA and the European Union, and holds an Aerospace Engineering degree from TU Braunschweig, where he specialized in Space Debris and Space Traffic Management.

Today, Jonas contributes his broad expertise and exceptional operational capabilities to both SFDY and our sister brand Levion Materials – making him an essential part of both crews.

Welcome to SFDY, Jonas!
